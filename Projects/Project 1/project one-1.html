Jason Elquist Project 1

Website: https://www.utah.com/nationalparks/

  1. What was your first impression of this site?
    At first glance, I thought the page was very visually appealing. The links all seem to work and are loaded with information. My one thing I didn’t like was the fact that they have pop-up ads. For me, that is not a good look for a government or a big corporation website.

  2. How does this site organize the flow of information? (Do they have links on the left and text on the right? Some other scheme? No scheme?)
    The website has links on the top and throughout the website to help users get the information they need or want.

  3. Does the site structure make sense or does it confuse you? Why?
    Yes, the structure makes sense, and it is very straightforward.

  4. Does the site graphics/layout/multimedia enhance the site or detract from it? Why?
    Yes, it has a picture of a woman hiking with her dog when you first go to the link. Throughout the site, they are good about having graphics to enhance the visual appeal.

  5. How long did it take for the home page to load? Was the load time reasonable?
    I feel like the homepage loads in a reasonable amount of time.

  6. Was the text color and size readable? If a background color or image was used, did it enhance or detract from the site?
    Yes, I feel like the colors made it so anyone could read it, from the young to the old.

  7. Did the site have a navigation bar or other navigation tool? Was the navigation well done?
    Yes and yes. I had no issues navigating the information.

  8. Did the site have a color scheme or look and feel that was repeated on all the pages?
    Yes, the color scheme felt very earthy, and it stayed consistent throughout the site.

  9. How does the site respond to smaller window sizes (responsive design)?
    I had no issues using it on my phone or computer.

  10. What does this site offer that was unusual or very well done?
    I felt like the site was very cookie-cutter.
